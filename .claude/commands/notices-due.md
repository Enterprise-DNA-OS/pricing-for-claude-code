---
description: Customers still to be told about a price change, with the date their terms require notice by.
---

Run: `npm run price -- notices-due`

Order by notify-by date. A late row cannot meet its notice period: say so and suggest moving the effective date or agreeing it with the customer.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency and margins as percentages. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here.
