---
description: Approve or decline a discount request.
---

Run: `npm run price -- decide <request-id> --decision=approved|declined --note= --actor=`

The person who asked cannot decide. If approved and the price should continue, follow with /special.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency and margins as percentages. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here.
