description: Where invoiced prices fell below what each customer was entitled to pay.

Run: `npm run price -- leakage`

Rank by money given away. Name the likely cause: an expired special still honoured, a rep discount, or an old price in the ERP.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency and margins as percentages. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here.
