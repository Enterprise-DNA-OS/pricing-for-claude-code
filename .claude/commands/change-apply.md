---
description: Write an approved price change into the price lists from its effective date.
---

Run: `npm run price -- change-apply <change> --actor=`

It stops while any customer has not been notified in time. Never work around that: move the date or record the agreement.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency and margins as percentages. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here.
