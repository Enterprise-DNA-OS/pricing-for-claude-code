---
description: Draft the price change letter for one customer.
---

Run: `npm run price -- draft-notice <change> <customer> --actor=`

It is blocked until the change is approved, while the notice period cannot be met, or with no email on file. Give the operator the file path. After they send it, record it with /notice.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency and margins as percentages. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here.
