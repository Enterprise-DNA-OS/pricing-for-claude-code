# Pricefx target research

Checked 14 October 2026. New registry family: price management. Industry: manufacturers, wholesalers and distributors.

Annual bill: Pricefx publishes no price list. Its own learning-centre article, archived on 30 May 2024 and no longer on the live site, put the subscription at US$100,000 to US$3.5 million a year on revenue under management, with implementation from US$100,000 ([archived article](https://web.archive.org/web/20240530064526/https://www.pricefx.com/learning-center/how-much-does-pricing-software-cost/)). That is a historical vendor statement, not a verified customer invoice, so the page and README name no figure.

Scores: annual bill 5, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 4, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 4. Total 24/30. NZ and AU distributors run price files, quantity breaks and per-customer special prices, usually in the ERP and spreadsheets. Search volume was not measured. Rivals considered: Zilliant, PROS, Vendavo and Flintfox by Enable, all quoted privately.

The scope is the price book and its weekly work: costs, list prices and breaks, specials, margin floors, price changes with notice, discount approvals and leakage from sales history. It excludes price optimisation models, quoting, rebates (see the rebates build), ERP write-back and any customer portal.
